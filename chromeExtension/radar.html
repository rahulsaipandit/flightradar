FIND FLIGHT
Flight status is powered by AeroDataBox on RapidAPI. To get one working:
  1. Sign in to RapidAPI and open the AeroDataBox page above.
  2. Open its Pricing tab and click Subscribe on a plan — having a RapidAPI account/key alone isn't enough, each API needs its own subscription or every call fails with "not subscribed".
  3. Flight status is a paid Tier 2 endpoint, so the free Basic plan alone won't unlock it — pick a paid plan.
  4. Copy your key from the page's Endpoints tab, then paste it into ⚙ Settings here.
